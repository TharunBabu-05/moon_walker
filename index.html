    FIELD GUIDE

    Find your footing.

    Three viewpoints, from a single footprint to the whole Moon.

    1 · First person
    Look through the astronaut’s eyes. Click for mouse look, or drag.
    2 · Astronaut
    Follow your astronaut. Drag to orbit; scroll to move the camera closer.
    3 · Orbit
    Explore every side of the Moon. Scroll out to see Earth, or use the Earth + Moon button.
    WASD / arrow keys
    Walk in the direction of the camera. Shift to lope. Space to jump.
    V / H / Esc
    Cycle views / hide the interface / release the mouse.

    E enters or exits your nearby buggy. W / S accelerate and reverse, A / D steer, Space brakes, and Shift raises the speed limit. Stop before exiting. Use CREW to host or join an expedition. M or the local map opens the lunar atlas. Choose Visit research station to enter the concept habitat.

    Jumps, rover flight, and loose dust use 1.62 m/s² gravity. The walkable highlands are an imagined landscape with detailed Poly Haven rock models and desaturated ground materials; the globe uses NASA LRO imagery.
